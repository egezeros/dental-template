# Research notes: aesthetic and vet clinics (raw)

Research date: 2026-10-22. Researcher: Claude Code (automated), for the agency's sales demo.
Criteria are the same as for the dental leads (`research/RESEARCH-NOTES.md`): **popular** (strong
ratings, active Instagram, local listings) but with a **weak online presence** (no website, an
outdated or single-page site, or no online booking). Chains that already have modern sites with
online booking were skipped.

## Method and limits — read this first

- **Only the web-search tool worked.** As in the dental round, this cloud session blocks direct page
  loads (Google Maps, Instagram, 2GIS, Yandex and the businesses' own domains), so nobody opened
  a page. Every fact comes from a search-result summary attributed to the cited URL.
- **No Google Maps rating could be retrieved for any of the six.** 2GIS and Yandex carry very few
  ratings for Baku clinics (usually 0–13), so ratings say little here. **Popularity rests on
  Instagram following and activity**, plus how many local directories list the business. This is
  weaker evidence than the dental round's Google ratings. Check each Google Maps listing by hand
  before outreach.
- Follower and post counts come from undated search snippets and may be stale.
- "No website" means none showed up in name, domain or `site:` searches. That is an absence of
  results, not proof. For Tarvet and Myvet the domain check was weak: the search tool dropped the
  `OR` operator.
- Website quality (speed, mobile layout, SSL) could not be tested, because pages could not be loaded.
- Effort: about 83 searches in Azerbaijani, Russian and English (about 41 per niche, by two research
  sub-agents), then 6 spot-checks of my own, one per pick. The spot-checks confirmed that each
  business exists, its name, Instagram account and size, and the address conflicts listed below.

Legend: **V** = two or more sources agree · **V1** = one source · **C** = sources conflict ·
**OLD** = dated · **NF** = not found.

- Not enough evidence: Vet Line, Skyvet, Cosmo Vet, Vet`n`Roll, Petty Paws, Baku Vet Clinic, Zoovet.
